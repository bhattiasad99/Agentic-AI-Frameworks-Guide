# Community Code of Conduct

This project exists to help people learn in public and make evidence-based engineering decisions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Welcome corrections and distinguish evidence from preference.
- Critique tools, workflows, and claims—not people.
- Disclose relevant commercial or project affiliations.
- Give maintainers time to review volunteer contributions.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Spam, undisclosed promotion, artificial engagement, or deceptive claims.
- Publishing another person’s private information.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion after a maintainer request.
- Submitting unsafe commands or malicious links.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.
